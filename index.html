01 — THE SYSTEM

A terminal, not a dashboard.

Northpoint is one screen for an entire operation: the protocol you follow before the open, every trade you take, the funded accounts you are climbing, the portfolio the money turns into, and the phase of the plan you are actually living. Nothing here is decorative — if a number shows up, something you did produced it.

What it does

It measures you against the plan you wrote that morning — your risk, your stop, your target — and not against your P&L. A losing day traded by the book is a professional day. A winning day that broke the rules is a liability, and the terminal will say so.

What it refuses to do

Breaches are detected on their own and cannot be deleted. The arithmetic of an account never rounds in your favour. A phase you have not paid for stays locked. The terminal is not on your side — it is on the side of the version of you that wrote the plan.

Six rules the software actually enforces

R — 01

The window is the edge

Prepare before 7:25, decide once the opening range has formed, be flat before 8:30. Anything logged outside that window is flagged. The edge is a slice of the clock, not the whole day.

R — 02

Thesis before entry

You write why you are entering before you enter, then mark whether you took it. If the checklist gives no valid setup, the best trade of the day is no trade — and that gets logged too.

R — 03

The daily cap is arithmetic

Maximum trades, stop and target are set before the open. Every trade is measured against that day's plan — not against a rule you rewrote after the fact.

R — 04

A breach cannot be erased

Overtrading, trading outside the window, moving a stop, revenge after a loss, adding after hitting target. They are computed from your own log and they stay.

R — 05

Trading never pays the rent

Cash flow is its own screen so you can see, in writing, whether the month depends on a trade. If it does, you do not have a business: you have a dangerous job.

R — 06

Every payout has a destination

Money you collect is split the day it lands — portfolio, own account, more accounts, living, free. Not deciding is also a decision, and it is the expensive one.

What this is not

It is not a signal service

Nobody tells you what to buy. The terminal holds you to what you said you would do.

It is not a broker

It does not touch your money and it places no orders. You trade where you already trade.

It does not promise returns

The arithmetic here describes what a payout costs, never what you will earn.

It does not live in the cloud unless you ask

Your journal stays in your browser by default. Syncing is a switch you turn on, not a condition of entry.

Discipline is not a feeling. It is something you can count.

02 — THE ARITHMETIC

An account is $6,100 of profit. That is the whole game.

A funded account is not a vibe, it is a number with three legs. The terminal knows those legs, detects on its own which one you are standing on, and moves the bar with your real trades. Nothing is marked by hand, and a leg you have cleared never un-clears.

LEG 01

$3,000 · the evaluation

$50,000 account → $53,000

The first three thousand of profit. When you get there the account resets to its starting balance and you are funded. The terminal notices on its own — you do not tell it.

LEG 02

$2,100 · the buffer

$50,000 → $52,100

The cushion the firm demands before releasing a single dollar. It is the leg where most accounts die: you are already funded, still not paid, and patience runs out.

LEG 03

$1,000 · the payout

$52,100 → $53,100

The maximum they will hand over per cycle. Getting there is 100%. The bar in the terminal is that: $3,000 + $2,100 + $1,000 = $6,100.

The day, in order

01

Prepare · 7:00 – 7:25

Overnight levels, the session that is holding the tape, the news that matters. It ends with one thing written down: what you are risking today.

02

Decide · the range has formed

From 7:30 to 7:45 the opening range builds. Only then does the checklist run: either there is a valid setup or the day is over before it started.

03

Write the thesis

Direction, level, invalidation, target. Before entering, not after. This is what separates a trade from a hunch.

04

Execute and log

Time, size, entry, exit, setup. If it isn't written down, the session never happened.

05

Flat before 8:30

The window closes. What comes after is not your edge — it is your boredom asking for a favour.

04 — THE WHEEL

Where every dollar you collect goes.

The payout arrives and the clock starts. Money without a destination gets spent — so the terminal asks for the amount and splits it on the spot into five places, with percentages that set themselves from the phase you are in. If you want to move them, they move; the wheel always closes at 100.

The five destinations

100% OF EVERY PAYOUT, THE DAY IT LANDS

Built on two frameworks that have been around longer than any of us: the 6 Jars system and Profit First. Their real insight is not the percentages — it is that the split happens before you look at your balance, not after.

What each one is for

Portfolio · the ten-year sheet

Bought to hold, valued with live prices. This is the slice that stops being trading and starts being wealth.

Own account · your capital

The only money whose return is yours whole. It starts small on purpose: this is where mistakes cost your own dollars.

More accounts · the ladder

Evaluations and resets. It is high early on and drops to zero at the top — the accounts are the ladder, never the destination.

Living · rent, food, the tax you owe

Profit First's contribution: tax comes off the top, before you feel rich. It is not your money and it never was.

Free · what is left

Once the other four are covered. Not guilt — a number. A plan with no room to enjoy anything gets abandoned by March.

The percentages move with the phase

Phase 0 feeds the ladder. Phase VII feeds the portfolio and nothing else. You do not have to remember: the terminal does.

The payout is not the win. Where it lands is.

05 — THE PLAN

From $0 to the house, in eight phases.

Not a wish list — the arithmetic of how you get there. Each phase carries what has to be true to leave it, what a month costs at that standard of living, and the rule that holds it up. The toys have gates: no car above 5% of net worth, no house above 25%. That is the difference between buying the toy and being able to keep it.

How a phase is read

The ticket · where the income comes from

How many funded accounts and what size, what your own account returns in percent and in dollars, and how much goes into the portfolio each month. Straight arithmetic — the accounts pay 2% of their size per cycle, and that is the whole ceiling.

The exit criteria

Net worth, portfolio balance, payouts collected, clean streaks with no breaches. The terminal checks them against your real numbers — you do not get to mark them yourself.

The month at that level

What life costs in that phase, itemised. Rent, food, health, golf, travel. Income that does not cover the month is not income: it is a loan from the next one.

The eight gates — projection under sustained discipline

$10K → $150M · 2027 – 2056 · LOG SCALE

A logarithmic scale, because on a linear one the first four phases would be a flat line on the floor — and those are the phases that decide whether the rest ever happens. The years are a projection under sustained discipline, not a promise.

The eight

0

The base · $0 – 10K

That the system exists — not the money. One account paying, one more behind it in evaluation, zero debt.

I

The cushion · 10K – 50K

Stop being fragile: a bad run is no longer an emergency. Expenses rise at most half as fast as income.

II

The first paycheck · 50K – 250K

The first asset that earns without you. Trading never pays the rent — if the month depends on a trade, it is a dangerous job.

III

The first million · 250K – 1M

Money starts working harder than you do. No car above 5% of net worth — and no, the G does not fit yet.

IV

Freedom · 1M – 5M

Passive income covers your life. You work because you want to. The house you live in never exceeds 25%.

V

The estate · 5M – 15M

It is no longer about earning more: it is about not losing it. Nothing concentrated above 20%.

VI

The house · 15M – 50M

The house is not bought with your net worth, but with what it produces. You sign when it costs under 25%.

VII

The summit · 50M – 150M

The money is no longer for you: it is for what you leave behind. Anything costing over 10% a year to keep is rented, not bought.
