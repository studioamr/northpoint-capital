NORTHPOINT · THE PLAN02 / 12

An account is $6,100 of profit.
That is the whole game.

LEG 01 · THE EVALUATION$3,000 $50,000 → $53,000. Pass it and the account resets, funded. The terminal detects it on its own.
LEG 02 · THE BUFFER$2,100 $50,000 → $52,100. The cushion the firm demands before releasing a dollar. Where most accounts die.
LEG 03 · THE PAYOUT$1,000 $52,100 → $53,100. The maximum per cycle — 2% of the account’s size. Getting here is 100%.

$3,000 + $2,100 + $1,000 = $6,100 — and the payout ceiling is what makes the arithmetic honest: more income means more accounts or bigger ones. There is no third way.

The eight gates.

A logarithmic scale, because on a linear one the first four phases would be a flat line on the floor — and those are the phases that decide whether the rest ever happens.

The years and figures in this plan are projections under sustained discipline over decades — not a forecast and not a guarantee. Prop-firm arithmetic reflects the rules of the firms and says nothing about the odds of clearing them: most people who try funded accounts lose money.
